# Student Reflective Guidance and Self-Review Brief

## Purpose

Student Reflective Guidance and Self-Review is a **future-facing learner layer** built on top of the core platform. It is intended to help students make better use of their exploration by turning recent activity into **clear, category-neutral next steps, recap support, and low-stakes review prompts**.

The core product remains a **path-based conceptual exploration and teacher-support system**. This capability extends learner value; it does **not** reposition the platform as a standalone diagnostic or mastery engine.

---

## Why This Matters

The exploration model is strong for discovery, curiosity, and conceptual movement. This layer matters if we want the product to become more useful between teacher interactions and more valuable for direct-to-learner use.

If done well, it could:
- make exploration feel more purposeful
- improve continuation and return behavior
- help students reflect on what they covered
- support light reinforcement without heavy testing
- increase the practical value of the product for independent study

---

## Core Product Role

This capability should be positioned internally as:
- a **learner-support layer**
- a **reflection and next-step layer**
- a **bounded self-review capability**

It should **not** be treated as the main product identity.

---

## What the System Does

Using recent exploration behavior, revisits, selections, and low-stakes checks, the system may eventually provide:
- short exploration recaps
- reflective prompts
- next-question suggestions
- reinforcement suggestions
- low-stakes self-review prompts
- re-entry guidance when returning to a topic

All learner-facing outputs must remain **supportive, actionable, and category-neutral**.

---

## What the System Is Not

This capability is **not**:
- a formal diagnostic engine
- a mastery judgment system
- a grading or certification mechanism
- a substitute for teacher judgment
- a student-visible category framework

It should never speak as if it definitively knows what a learner does or does not understand.

---

## Product Principles

The learner-facing experience should be:
- **category-neutral**: no visible analytic dimensions or framework labels
- **reflective, not judgmental**: prompts should guide, not evaluate
- **low-stakes**: more like coaching than testing
- **grounded in real behavior**: based on actual path activity, not generic advice
- **honest about uncertainty**: suggestions should remain cautious and non-authoritative
- **actionable**: each output should help the learner do something next

---

## Strategic Value

This capability is most strategically useful if it strengthens two product motions:

1. **Direct-to-learner value**
   - makes the product feel more helpful, sticky, and self-guided
2. **Teacher-supported value**
   - helps students continue useful study between teacher interactions without confusing learner support with teacher interpretation

---

## Initial Success Criteria

This capability is worth expanding only if it improves real behavior, such as:
- more productive follow-up exploration
- stronger return-to-topic behavior
- higher use of recap / next-step prompts
- better reinforcement activity
- positive learner feedback on helpfulness and tone

---

## Guardrails

Do not expand claims unless evidence supports them. Watch for:
- false confidence
- over-interpretation of learner understanding
- guidance being mistaken for formal assessment
- accidental exposure of the hidden analytic framework

---

## Recommended Internal Positioning Sentence

**Student Reflective Guidance and Self-Review is a future learner-facing support layer that helps students reflect on what they explored, revisit ideas that may need reinforcement, and choose useful next steps through category-neutral guidance and low-stakes review support.**
